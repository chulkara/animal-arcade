# Third-party 3D asset credits — experimental tiger model swap

The assets are licensed third-party work, not original Noah's Arkade models. Their creators do not endorse this project.

- **CGJAY1111**, [White Tiger (RIGGED ANIMATED)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/white-tiger-rigged-animated-9dd099d283e54f99b7cbd40b531b1a29) — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. High-detail mesh, textures, rig and original Idle. Adaptations in this work: amber-orange diffuse recolor and transferred Howl animation.
- **Blender Artist**, [Tiger](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tiger-67bbedd727a047869ef7c7b608445484) — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Source of the previously transferred Howl motion.
- **Amil / francescolima74**, [Running Tiger](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/running-tiger-fc2c7fd74dd04e39935803b884c77258) — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Native Run animation and separate low-poly runner model.
