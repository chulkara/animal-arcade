The assets are licensed third-party work, not original Noah's Arkade models. Their creators do not endorse this project.

- **CGJAY1111**, [White Tiger (RIGGED ANIMATED)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/white-tiger-rigged-animated-9dd099d283e54f99b7cbd40b531b1a29) — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. ## Great White Shark 3D model (playable shark)
- File: `assets/models/shark/great_white_shark.glb` (bundled, loaded automatically).
- Creator: **Mateus Schwaab** ([Mehrus on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/Mehrus)),
  [Great White Shark](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/great-white-shark-69d7911ee12f44889c6ae9c9c2a99ba0).
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
- Used unmodified as a file: mesh, textures, rig and its six clips (Swim, SwimFast,
  Bite1, Bite2, Hit, Hit01). Adaptations in this work: the game's own procedural
  motion layered at runtime (hover, tail lash, breach, knockout roll, victory),
  the arena's matte material settings and gold/aura tints.
- The creator does not endorse this project. Credited in-game under SETTINGS → CREDITS
  and on the animal select screen.
